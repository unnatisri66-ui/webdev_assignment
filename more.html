What I Am Exploring

"Still figuring things out. And that's the exciting part."

Building something of my own

I have always wanted to build something of my own.

I have ideas, questions and possibilities running through my mind, but I am equally aware that having an idea and building a successful venture are two very different things.

I want to understand what makes an idea worth pursuing, how to identify meaningful problems, how to validate solutions, and how to turn an idea into a sustainable business.

An emerging interest in Venture Capital

My involvement in the fundraising process at RiseUpp introduced me to a side of entrepreneurship that I had not explored closely before.

I became curious about the people and institutions that back businesses, the decisions behind investments and the process through which promising companies are identified.

Lately, I have been exploring venture capital firms, studying their portfolios, understanding their investment theses and observing the kinds of businesses they choose to back.

I am particularly interested in understanding due diligence, business evaluation, market potential and what makes a venture investment-worthy.

It is an area I would love to explore much more deeply and potentially build my career around.

Beyond building a business

My long-term ambition goes beyond simply starting a company.

I want to build something that contributes meaningfully to society. Not through philanthropy or conventional charity, but through an enterprise that solves real problems, creates opportunities and contributes to the country's development.

I am particularly drawn to the idea of nation-building through entrepreneurship, where the impact of a venture can be seen in the lives it improves, the opportunities it creates and the problems it helps solve.

What lies ahead?

I am still exploring different ideas, industries and opportunities. I don't have all the answers yet, and I don't think I need to.

For now, I want to keep learning, meet people who challenge my thinking, understand how businesses operate and experiment with ideas.

I don't just want to find a career. I want to discover what I can build, why it matters and how far I can take it.
