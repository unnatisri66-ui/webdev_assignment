Exploring My Own Orbit

I am Unnati Srivastava, a curious mind, an enthusiastic learner and, above all, an observer.

I have always been fascinated by how things work, why people make certain decisions, and how seemingly small ideas can turn into something much bigger. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar subjects, asking questions and understanding the world beyond what I already know.

My interests naturally gravitate towards entrepreneurship and businesses. I am particularly fascinated by the journey of building something from scratch, understanding the problems worth solving, bringing people and resources together, and creating something that makes a meaningful difference.

I believe the best way to learn is to get involved, take responsibility and figure things out along the way. My experiences so far have taken me through different roles, responsibilities and environments, each introducing me to something new.

I am still exploring, experimenting and figuring out where my interests truly lie. And I quite enjoy being at this stage of the journey.
