Responsibilities & Leadership

"Leadership begins with showing up."

House Captain

Mother Teresa Higher Secondary School | 2023

One of my most memorable leadership experiences was serving as the Head House Captain of St. John House.

Being a house captain was much more than holding a position. It meant representing a team, coordinating people, managing activities and ensuring that everyone worked towards a common goal.

My responsibilities included

Representing my house in school leadership meetings and discussions.
Coordinating with other house captains for inter-house activities.
Organising and managing house-level events and competitions.
Coordinating with teachers and students during school functions.
Representing St. John House in inter-school competitions and activities.
Helping maintain discipline and ensuring smooth execution of activities.

What I learnt

This role introduced me to the practical side of leadership. I learnt how to coordinate with people with different perspectives, delegate responsibilities, manage activities and take ownership of outcomes.

More importantly, I understood that leadership is not just about being at the front. It is about making sure that everyone moves forward together.

The experience also gave me an early opportunity to understand the importance of teamwork, communication and accountability.
