The environments were very different, but both taught me something valuable.

This experience has opened up an entirely new area of exploration for me.

Vaibhav Pahariya & Company

Administrator | September 2025 – March 2026

Chartered Accountants

This was my first professional experience, and perhaps one of the most memorable learning experiences of my journey so far.

I entered the firm with practically zero professional experience. I was the youngest person there, and initially, my biggest intention was simply to observe, understand and learn.

What started as an opportunity to explore the professional world gradually turned into an experience filled with responsibilities, relationships and valuable lessons.

What I worked on

  • Managing day-to-day office administration and operations.
  • Coordinating with clients and handling their queries.
  • Assisting with MCA compliance filings and company incorporations.
  • Supporting GST return preparation and financial documentation.
  • Managing Digital Signature Certificate documentation.
  • Coordinating tasks and workflows across the team.

What I took away

Beyond the technical and administrative work, this experience taught me how to communicate with people, understand professional expectations, take ownership and work with a team.

I also learnt that being new to something does not have to be a disadvantage. Sometimes, entering a new environment with no preconceived notions gives you the freedom to learn from everything around you.

I remain grateful to the people at the firm, my seniors and colleagues, for the opportunities, encouragement and relationships that made this experience so meaningful.
